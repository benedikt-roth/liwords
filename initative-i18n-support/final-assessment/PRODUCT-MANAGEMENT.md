# Product Management assessment

Product Management owns continuity of current user journeys, accessibility, and fidelity to feature and domain semantics. The assessed design is the first English-only iteration in the [initiative specification](../feature-specification.md). I reviewed the [domain contexts](../../architecture/domain/contexts.md), [domain model](../../architecture/domain/model.md), [domain vocabulary](../../architecture/domain/language.md), [workflows](../../architecture/domain/workflows.md), and relevant application source at `b5343a5e3d2c9e93e2ec3e6f4cb319749f59b2dd`.

## Recommendation

Seek maintainer alignment and verify the catalog on existing build paths, then start a parity-preserving extraction pilot. Keep `en-US` as the sole production language and the signed-out fallback. Track the full first-iteration scope in the acceptance inventory and migrate it in independently reviewable slices. Use one authored language file across renderers and future locales, including owned help/legal prose, with semantic keys and section-level review. Runtime projections can be derived where the build requires them, subject to confirmation of the one-file boundary. Confirm that boundary and the badge-content policy before migrating those surfaces.

Current regional presentation and supported sessions need explicit decisions before any migration changes them. Preserve existing per-surface formatting and avoid forced reloads during active play or unsaved edits in the pilot. The draft's global `en-US` formatting and required old-tab reload remain open against the request for non-breaking changes. ## Findings that affect product scope

### The date-format policy changes real tasks

The tournament wizard, director metadata form, and admin tournament editor derive 12/24-hour mode from browser language. Registration orders its date-of-birth input fields from browser conventions. Public broadcast lists and scorecards render dates with the browser's default locale, while the league page uses its own US English formatting. A static `en-US` choice would change input interaction and visible hour/date/number conventions for some users, even if the timezone and submitted values stayed the same. [Hour preference helper](../../liwords-ui/src/utils/datetime.ts#L10) · [Tournament wizard](../../liwords-ui/src/tournament/tournament_wizard.tsx#L97) · [Director metadata](../../liwords-ui/src/tournament/director_tools/ghetto_tools/metadata.tsx#L84) · [Admin editor](../../liwords-ui/src/admin/tourney_editor.tsx#L146) · [Registration date ordering](../../liwords-ui/src/lobby/register.tsx#L55) · [Broadcast schedule](../../liwords-ui/src/broadcasts/BroadcastsList.tsx#L15) · [Game scorecard](../../liwords-ui/src/gameroom/scorecard.tsx#L206) · [League windows](../../liwords-ui/src/leagues/league_page.tsx#L90)

I reproduced the corresponding `Intl` behavior in Node using the same presentation options and a fixed `Europe/Berlin` timezone. At `2026-03-29T01:30Z`, `en-US` produced `Mar 29, 2026, 3:30 AM` and `de-DE` produced `29. März 2026, 3:30`. Date parts came out month/day/year and day/month/year respectively. The hour controls resolve to 12-hour and 24-hour, and grouping 1200 yields `1,200` and `1.200`. This establishes a behavior difference at the formatting primitive. Full browser and form round-trip validation belong to the implementation slice.

Keep present conventions per surface in a parity release. Keep device timezones separate from interface language and preserve explicit contexts such as the six-zone list in league reminder emails. If the requester approves locale-driven formatting, the maintainer should review a separate change with before/after examples and date/clock input tests. Keep game-clock duration displays and timing logic consistent with current gameplay. [League reminder timezones](../../pkg/league/league_notify.go#L56) · [Game timer](../../liwords-ui/src/gameroom/simple_timer.tsx#L25)

## Coverage and acceptance by task

Use these checks alongside the [domain capability map](../../architecture/domain/contexts.md) and [initiative acceptance criteria](../feature-specification.md#acceptance-criteria). Track each surface as covered with evidence, excluded by the confirmed scope, or pending. Successful completion of the pilot covers its selected seams.

| Domain context | Representative parity check |
| --- | --- |
| Gameplay and Rules | Complete real-time and correspondence play, including valid/invalid challenges and reconnect as player/observer. Preserve eligibility, rack censorship, lexicon and tile encoding, coordinates, clocks, ending reason, scoring, and recorded events. |
| Matchmaking | Create an open seek and directed match request. Accept as an eligible opponent, decline or reject as ineligible, and preserve follow/block, rating range and choice of game rules. |
| Tournaments and Clubs | Director starts a round, eligible entrant checks in and readies, reporter enters an IRL result, and director downloads standings and printable scorecards. Preserve authorization, time inputs, pairing/bye/forfeit rules, output, and linked competition identities. |
| Leagues | Register, receive a reminder, play correspondence, and inspect standings and placement. Preserve season registration/closure deadlines, explicit timezone display, recipient and linked season, and adjudication behavior. |
| Ratings and Statistics | Verify rated/casual games update the same eligible pools and profile/public values. The rating input, standings rank and analysis mistake index retain their own meanings. |
| Puzzles | Get a puzzle under the selected game lexicon, submit a wrong attempt and then solve, or reveal the answer. Preserve unresolved, solved and given-up states, rating effects, source-game links and anonymous practice. |
| Analysis | An eligible user requests completed-game analysis and sees quota/status/conditional success feedback. Keep worker claim/heartbeat/result protocol, ownership, and local WASM simulation independent of labels. |
| Study and Annotation | Import/export GCG, edit an owned GameDocument, mark annotation done, load the owned embed and browse a collection. Preserve game end reason, author content, collection/chapter order, link paths and write permissions. |
| Broadcasts | Director retargets an event-owned broadcast slot, user follows it from a personal stream slot, and subscriber keeps the existing OBS URL/SSE output. Check builder, local preview, help page and help modal. |
| Identity and Access | Exercise registration, verification/resend, forgot password, login/logout/account switching and role visibility. Preserve links, email routing, access, private preference and any unverified/shared API response. |
| Community and Trust | Mix user and legacy/structured system chat, reconnect, delete and moderate. Keep search/discovery, ordering, notifications, review exclusions, and sanction boundaries. |
| Integrations and Entitlements | Keep provider links, organization titles, badge assignments/assets and application entitlement decisions. Verify relevant external consumers and webhook behavior without introducing localization into machine payloads. |

Also review main and embed document titles, accessibility and alternative text, library controls, CSS-generated labels, canvas and images, public help and legal content, the surrounding announcements UI, and transactional email templates. A development-only fixture can exercise interpolation, plurals, linked prose and text expansion across the dense game/director views and the PDF renderer. Verify that the production locale registry, preference writes and packaged locales support only `en-US`. Retain human names, authored messages, announcement bodies, organization titles and lexical data in their original forms. ## Cross-role conclusion

Product Management agrees with Software Engineering and Software Architecture on a small, parity-safe pilot, reviewed contract boundaries, and a single authored translation source with generated packaging subject to the scope decision. The engineering review identified logic coupled to English and checked format parity; the architecture review prioritized client compatibility, shared help resolution, and the current badge admin/metadata workflow. Product added task-based acceptance across all contexts and the explicit decisions on time presentation and reload. The assessment used source inspection and an `Intl` reproduction. These are proposed release gates and acceptance checks, with end-to-end implementation evidence still pending.
